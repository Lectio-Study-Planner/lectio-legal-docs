  3. You may look at your friends' schedules only for yourself. You may not pass them on, publish or collect them, or use them to stalk or harass anyone. Don't write anything in shared notes that your friends shouldn't see.
  4. You can withdraw a request and remove a friend at any time. Sharing then ends in both directions.
